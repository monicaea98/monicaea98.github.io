description: >
  Raw <strong>e-commerce datasets</strong> were cleaned, transformed, and merged to prepare them for analysis. 
  <strong>Exploratory data analysis</strong> was conducted to evaluate <strong>monthly revenue trends</strong>, 
  <strong>top revenue-generating product categories</strong>, <strong>customer purchase behavior</strong>, 
  and <strong>geographic sales distribution</strong>.

  Key <strong>business insights</strong> showed a consistent <strong>upward revenue trend</strong> over time, 
  a small group of categories generating a large share of total revenue, opportunities to improve 
  <strong>customer retention</strong>, and sales concentrated in a few key states. 
  <strong>Average Order Value</strong> remained relatively stable.

  Findings were presented in an <strong>interactive Tableau dashboard</strong> to support 
  <strong>data-driven decision-making</strong>.
