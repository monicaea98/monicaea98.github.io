  Raw e-commerce datasets were cleaned, transformed, and merged to prepare them for analysis. Exploratory data analysis was conducted to evaluate monthly revenue trends, top revenue-generating product categories, customer purchase behavior, and geographic sales distribution.

  Key business insights showed a consistent upward revenue trend over time, a small group of categories generating a large share of total revenue, opportunities to improve customer retention, and sales concentrated in a few key states. Average Order Value remained relatively stable. Findings were presented in an interactive Tableau dashboard to support data-driven decision-making.
