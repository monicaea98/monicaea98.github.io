description: >
  A relational <strong>book subscription service database</strong> was explored using 
  <strong>SQL queries</strong> to analyze <strong>user behavior</strong>, 
  <strong>book characteristics</strong>, and <strong>publishing trends</strong>. 
  The analysis focused on extracting insights to support the development of a 
  <strong>digital reading product</strong> in a growing online market.

  Key analytical tasks included evaluating <strong>books published after 2000</strong>, 
  measuring <strong>user engagement through ratings and reviews</strong>, identifying 
  <strong>top-performing publishers</strong> based on full-length publications, and determining 
  the <strong>highest-rated authors</strong> using minimum rating thresholds. 
  Additional analysis highlighted the impact of <strong>power users</strong> who contribute a large share 
  of written reviews.

  All insights were generated through <strong>advanced SQL aggregations</strong>, 
  <strong>joins across multiple tables</strong>, and <strong>data filtering techniques</strong>, 
  demonstrating strong ability to work directly with structured relational data. 
  Results were documented and interpreted to support <strong>data-driven product decisions</strong>.
