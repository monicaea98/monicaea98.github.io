description: >
  An interactive <strong>Personal Finance Analytics Dashboard</strong> built in 
  <strong>Power BI</strong> to monitor <strong>income</strong>, 
  <strong>expenses</strong>, <strong>profit</strong>, and 
  <strong>financial balance</strong> against predefined targets. 
  The project transforms raw financial data into 
  <strong>clear and actionable insights</strong> that support smarter financial 
  decision-making and long-term financial planning.

  The dashboard includes multiple analytical views focused on 
  <strong>financial performance tracking</strong>, including 
  <strong>income vs goals</strong>, <strong>expenses vs budget</strong>, 
  <strong>profitability analysis</strong>, and 
  <strong>balance growth monitoring</strong>. Interactive filters and KPI indicators 
  allow users to quickly evaluate whether financial performance is meeting expectations.

  <strong>Data modeling</strong> and <strong>DAX measures</strong> were used to create 
  dynamic calculations and performance metrics across different time periods and categories. 
  The dashboard also incorporates 
  <strong>monthly trend analysis</strong>, 
  <strong>category-level comparisons</strong>, and 
  <strong>target achievement tracking</strong> to identify financial patterns and 
  areas for improvement.

  Financial analysis revealed that 
  <strong>income generally remained close to target values</strong>, while certain periods 
  showed <strong>expenses exceeding planned budgets</strong>. 
  Profitability improved over time as revenue stabilized, and the overall balance demonstrated 
  <strong>consistent long-term growth</strong>.
